# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Week One

- Complete HR paperwork and benefits enrollment (see `health-benefits.md`)
- Receive equipment per `it-setup.md`
- Obtain badge access per `badge-access.md`
- Review the Code of Conduct (`code-of-conduct.md`)

## Week Two

- Meet with manager to set 30/60/90-day goals
- Complete mandatory security training referenced in `data-security.md`

## First Month

New hires are not eligible for the remote-work stipend described in `home-office-stipend.md`
until they complete their 30-day check-in with HR.
