# Equipment Policy

New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals during onboarding — see
`it-setup.md` for the provisioning timeline.

## Refresh Cycle

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if a hardware fault is confirmed by IT.

## Personal Use

Company equipment may be used for incidental personal tasks but must remain compliant with
`device-security.md` at all times, including when used for personal purposes.
