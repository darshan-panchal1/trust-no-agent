# Conflict of Interest Policy

Employees must disclose any financial interest in a vendor, competitor, or client that could
reasonably influence their business decisions at Meridian.

## Disclosure Process

Disclosures are made annually and whenever a new potential conflict arises, through the same
ethics line referenced in `code-of-conduct.md`.

## Vendor Relationships

An employee may not participate in selecting or approving a vendor in which they, or an
immediate family member, hold a financial interest — see `vendor-approval.md` for the standard
selection process this restriction applies to.
