# Badge Access Policy

Physical badge access is provisioned on an employee's first day, tied to their office location
and team.

## Restricted Areas

Server rooms and the executive floor require additional sign-off from Facilities and, for the
server room specifically, from IT Security.

## Lost Badges

Report a lost badge to Facilities immediately. A temporary badge is issued same-day; the lost
badge is deactivated within one hour of the report.
