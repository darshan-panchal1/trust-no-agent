# Travel Safety Policy

Employees traveling internationally must register their itinerary with the Security team at
least 3 business days before departure.

## High-Risk Destinations

Travel to a destination on the current High-Risk list requires additional sign-off from Security
in addition to the standard approval described in `travel.md`. The High-Risk list is maintained
separately and reviewed quarterly.

## Emergency Contact

Every traveler must designate an emergency contact in the travel portal before a trip can be
booked. Security maintains a 24-hour line for travelers in an active incident — see
`incident-response.md`.
