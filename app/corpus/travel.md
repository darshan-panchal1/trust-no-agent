# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All flights and hotels are booked through the Meridian travel portal. Bookings outside the
portal are reimbursed only at the portal's equivalent price.

## Approval Thresholds

Domestic trips under $2,000 total cost require only direct manager sign-off.

**International trips, or any trip exceeding $2,000 total cost, require approval from a manager
at Director level or above.** This threshold exists regardless of the traveler's own seniority —
a Director-level traveler still needs a second Director-or-above approver for their own
qualifying trip.

See `procurement.md` for the definition of seniority tiers, since "Director level or above" is
defined there, not here.

## Booking Class

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights over 6 hours
with manager approval. Business class requires Director-level approval regardless of flight
length.
