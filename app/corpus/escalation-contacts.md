# Escalation Contacts

If the standard incident channel described in `incident-response.md` is unavailable, escalate
directly:

- Security on-call: available 24/7 via the intranet directory
- IT on-call: for device or access issues outside business hours
- HR duty officer: for conduct or harassment reports requiring immediate attention

## When to Escalate Directly

Direct escalation is for active incidents only — an ongoing data exposure, a lost device with
Restricted data, or a safety concern during travel (see `travel-safety.md`). Non-urgent matters
should use the standard channels described in the relevant policy document.
