# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval. Fully remote
arrangements require Director-level sign-off.

## Core Hours

All employees, remote or in-office, are expected to be reachable between 10am and 3pm in their
local timezone, coordinated with their team's primary timezone.

## Equipment

Remote employees are eligible for the standard equipment allowance described in
`equipment.md`, and may also qualify for the stipend in `home-office-stipend.md`.
