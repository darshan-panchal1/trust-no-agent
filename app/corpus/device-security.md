# Device Security Policy

All company laptops ship with full-disk encryption and mandatory screen lock after 5 minutes of
inactivity.

## Loaner Laptops for Travel

Employees traveling to certain regions must use a loaner laptop with no local company data,
provisioned by IT before departure — see `international-travel.md` for when this applies.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported immediately through `incident-response.md`. IT will
remote-wipe the device once reported.
