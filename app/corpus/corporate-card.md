# Corporate Card Policy

Employees at Senior Manager level and above are issued a corporate card for business expenses.
Employees below Senior Manager level submit expenses for reimbursement instead (see
`expense-reporting.md`).

## Card Limits

- Standard corporate card: $5,000 monthly limit
- Travel-specific card (issued for extended trips): $10,000 monthly limit

## Misuse

Any personal charge on a corporate card must be repaid within 5 business days of the statement
date. Repeated misuse results in card revocation and is reported to the employee's manager and
to HR.
