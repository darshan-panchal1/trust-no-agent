# Parental Leave Policy

Meridian offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for a
secondary caregiver, for birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Eligibility

Available to employees who have completed 6 months of continuous employment at the time leave
begins.

## Coordination with Sick Leave

Medical recovery time related to childbirth is coordinated through the extended illness process
in `sick-leave.md` and runs concurrently with, not in addition to, parental leave.

## Return to Work

Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed their prior role or an equivalent role at
the same level and compensation.
