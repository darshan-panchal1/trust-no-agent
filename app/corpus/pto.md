# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per year during their first two years, increasing to 20
days from year three onward.

## Requesting PTO

PTO requests go through the HR portal and require manager approval. Requests of 5 or more
consecutive days should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the following calendar year. Days beyond that cap
are forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Unlimited PTO

Meridian does not offer an unlimited PTO policy. All PTO is accrued and tracked as described
above.
