# Anti-Harassment Policy

Meridian has zero tolerance for harassment based on protected characteristics, including but not
limited to race, gender, age, disability, and religion.

## Reporting

Reports may be made to a manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics line referenced in
`code-of-conduct.md`. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation subject to
disciplinary action.

## Investigation Timeline

HR aims to begin an investigation within 3 business days of a report and to communicate an
outcome to the reporter within 30 days, though complex cases may take longer.
