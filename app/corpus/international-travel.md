# International Travel Requirements

## Documentation

Employees are responsible for their own passport validity (minimum 6 months remaining) and any
required visas. Meridian does not reimburse expedited passport or visa fees except for
last-minute client-driven trips approved by a Director.

## Currency and Expenses

International per diem follows the same flat rate described in the current expense policy —
see `expenses-v2.md` — converted at the portal's daily exchange rate, not the traveler's own
card rate.

## Data Security Abroad

Loaner laptops are available for travel to certain regions per `device-security.md`. Check with
IT before departure if traveling to a region on the loaner-required list.
