# Sick Leave Policy

Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped at a fixed number of days per year for
short-term illness.

## Notification

Notify your manager as early as possible on the day of absence. No doctor's note is required
for absences of 2 days or fewer.

## Extended Illness

Absences beyond 5 consecutive days require a note from a healthcare provider and are coordinated
with HR under the extended leave process, which may overlap with `parental-leave.md`'s process
for medical leave related to childbirth.
