# Code of Conduct

All Meridian employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and
professionalism.

## Scope

This code applies at Meridian offices, client sites, company events, and in any
company-sponsored communication channel, including outside normal business hours at
company-sponsored events.

## Reporting Violations

Violations are reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics line. See
`anti-harassment.md` for the specific process covering harassment complaints, and
`conflict-of-interest.md` for financial or business-relationship conflicts.
