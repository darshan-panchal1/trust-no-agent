# Vendor Approval Process

Before engaging a new vendor, submit a Vendor Intake Form to Procurement. Procurement runs a
compliance check (insurance, tax status, conflict-of-interest screen) before any contract is
signed.

## Timeline

Standard vendor intake takes 5–10 business days. Expedited review (2–3 business days) is
available for purchases under $5,000 with VP sign-off on the expedite request itself.

## Existing Vendor Changes

Changes to an existing vendor contract (renewal, scope change, price change) go through the
original approver's tier, not a fresh intake.
