# Expense Reimbursement Policy (v2)

**This document supersedes `expenses-v1.md`, effective the date below.** Where the two
documents disagree, this version is authoritative.

## Per Diem Rates

Meridian Logistics reimburses employee travel meals at a flat per diem of **$65 USD per day**,
regardless of destination. Receipts are not required for per diem claims under this rate. This
replaces the previous $45/day rate.

## Submission Window

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports
submitted after this window are subject to manager approval on a case-by-case basis. This
requirement is unchanged from the prior policy.

## Eligible Categories

- Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, public transit)
- Meals during business travel (subject to the per diem rate above)
- Lodging, at actual cost, capped at $200/night domestic
- Client entertainment, with a business justification note

## Non-Reimbursable

Personal entertainment, alcohol purchased outside a client meal, and travel upgrades beyond
economy class are not reimbursable under this policy.

## Revision Note

The per diem increase reflects rising meal costs across Meridian's operating regions, reviewed
annually by Finance.
