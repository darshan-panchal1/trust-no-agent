# Home Office Stipend

Employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week are eligible for a one-time $500 home
office setup stipend, plus $50/month ongoing for internet and utilities.

## Claiming the Stipend

The one-time stipend is claimed as an expense category in the Finance portal, following the
same submission window described in the current expense policy (`expenses-v2.md`). The
ongoing $50/month is paid automatically through payroll once enrolled.

## Eligibility Review

Eligibility is reviewed if an employee's remote-work arrangement changes; see
`remote-work-policy.md` for the days-per-week threshold that determines eligibility.
