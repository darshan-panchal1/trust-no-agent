# Incident Response Policy

Security incidents (lost devices, suspected data exposure, phishing, policy violations
involving Restricted data) are reported immediately to the Security team.

## Reporting Channel

Reports go through the security hotline or the incident form, both linked from the intranet
home page. See `escalation-contacts.md` for direct contacts if the standard channel is
unavailable.

## Initial Response

Security acknowledges a report within 1 hour during business hours, or 4 hours outside business
hours, and begins triage immediately. Lost or stolen devices trigger an automatic remote wipe
per `device-security.md`.
