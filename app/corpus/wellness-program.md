# Wellness Program

Employees receive a $300 annual wellness reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness equipment,
or wellness apps.

## Claiming

Claimed the same way as other reimbursements, through the process in `expense-reporting.md`,
tagged with the wellness category.

## Mental Health

Meridian provides an Employee Assistance Program offering free, confidential counseling
sessions, available regardless of health plan enrollment status.
