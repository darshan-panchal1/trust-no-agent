# Procurement and Seniority Tiers

## Seniority Tiers

Meridian Logistics uses five seniority tiers, lowest to highest:

1. Associate
2. Manager
3. Senior Manager
4. Director
5. Vice President (VP)

Titles including "Senior Manager" are **below** Director level. Only titles at Director, VP, or
above count as "Director level or above" for any policy that uses that phrase (see
`travel.md` for one such policy).

## Purchase Authority by Tier

| Tier | Single-purchase authority |
|---|---|
| Associate | $500 |
| Manager | $2,000 |
| Senior Manager | $10,000 |
| Director | $50,000 |
| VP | $250,000 |

## Vendor Contracts

New vendor contracts require Director-level sign-off regardless of dollar value, since a
contract creates an ongoing obligation rather than a one-time purchase.
