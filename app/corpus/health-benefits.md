# Health Benefits

Meridian offers medical, dental, and vision coverage, effective the first of the month following
30 days of employment.

## Enrollment

Enrollment happens during onboarding (see `onboarding-checklist.md`) or during the annual open
enrollment period each November. Qualifying life events allow enrollment outside these windows.

## Dependents

Spouses, domestic partners, and children up to age 26 may be added as dependents at any
enrollment opportunity.
